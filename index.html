BIOWEEK

Tekken Tournament

Rules & Regulations

Tekken 8 · BIOWEEK

1 · Game & Settings

  • All matches are played on the latest version of Tekken 8, using the default match settings.
  • This is an in-person event.
  • Stages are chosen at random.

2 · Match Format

  • First to 3 (FT3) for most matches: the first player to win 3 rounds takes the match.
  • First to 5 (FT5) for the Upper Final, Lower Final, Grand Finals, Bracket Reset, and the Showmatch Grand Finals, Faculty Showmatch and Final Boss Match: the first player to win 5 rounds takes the match.
  • Each match is one continuous game with no rematch screen, so the same characters and stage are used for the whole match.

3 · Characters & Skins

  • You may choose any skin available in the game, as long as it is not suggestive.
  • Characters stay the same for the whole match.

4 · Controls

  • Special Style (LB / L1) is forbidden. Even though it is part of the game, we are all here to button mash!
  • Using Special Style counts as a warning.
  • You may bring your own controller. Tell the organizers before the tournament starts so we can check if it is compatible.
  • Turbo or macro controllers are not allowed.

5 · Conduct & Warnings

  • No bad words toward the opponent, staff or audience.
  • No trash talking. Maintain sportsmanship at all times, win or lose.
  • Cheering, screaming and shouting are fine, as long as they stay respectful and are not aimed at the opponent.
  • 3 warnings = automatic disqualification. Bad words and trash talking each count as a warning, and warnings carry over for the whole tournament.
  • Respect your opponent: shake hands (or GG) before and after.
  • No physical fights. No hitting, pushing or any physical contact, and not even threatening to. No throwing or slamming of controllers or equipment.

6 · No Pausing

  • You cannot pause the game mid-match, especially as a tactic.
  • A deliberate pause gives the round to your opponent and counts as a warning.
  • If you paused by accident, tell a judge right away. The judge decides if the round is replayed.

7 · Match Flow

  • Be at the station when your match is called. After 5 minutes of no-show, you forfeit.
  • Leaving or quitting mid-match counts as a forfeit.

8 · Disqualification

  • A player is disqualified for 3 warnings, cheating, serious unsportsmanlike behavior, or any physical altercation or threat of one. Their opponent advances automatically.

9 · Disputes

  • Raise any concern before the next game starts. The judges' decision is final.
